# Product

Two separate public sites for housing-society residents in Dombivli, India. They share no link, login or data.

## Garden City Seva (gardencityseva.com)
- **What:** maintenance complaints for Runwal Garden City: raise a problem, follow it until it is fixed.
- **Who:** residents, maintenance staff (plumbers, electricians, housekeeping) and society admins, mostly on phones, often outdoors or in corridors.
- **Homepage job:** get a resident to sign in with their mobile number, after showing how a complaint moves.
- **Tone:** a society signboard: plain, direct, readable at a glance.

## Runwal MyCity Residents (runwalltdfakepromises.com)
- **What:** a moderated public feed where residents document what was promised and what exists today.
- **Who:** owners and tenants, and anyone reading their posts.
- **Homepage job:** get a visitor to read residents' posts; residents then sign in to post.
- **Tone:** a careful record, in the residents' own voice. The page states no fact about the builder itself.

## Rules for both
- English, Hindi and Marathi, with the same content in each. English prevails on legal pages.
- Never invent a figure, testimonial or claim. Samples are labelled as samples.
- Touch targets of at least 44px, body text of at least 16px, usable without motion and without script.
