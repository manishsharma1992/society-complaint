Who can use it?

Residents of Runwal MyCity, Dombivli: owners and tenants. Sign in with your mobile number, tell us your flat, and the society admin approves you, usually within a day.

Is this the developer's app?

No. It is an independent app for residents. It is not run by, or affiliated with, Runwal or the builder.

Who sees my mobile number?

Nobody except the society admin, once, to check your sign-up. Other residents see names only, and feed posts never show who wrote them.

What gets published in the feed?

Factual, documented issues: what, where, when, and what was promised. Posts with names, faces, abuse or unproven accusations are not published.

Which languages does it support?

English, हिन्दी and मराठी. Choose at the top of any screen; the app remembers it on your phone.
