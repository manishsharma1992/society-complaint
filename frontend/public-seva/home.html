Who can use it?

Residents, maintenance staff and administrators of Runwal Garden City.

Who sees my complaint?

A complaint about your flat is seen by your household, the staff and the admins. A common-area complaint is seen by everyone in the society.

Who sees my mobile number?

Only the society admin who checks your sign-up. Other people see names, never numbers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in the browser on any phone, in English, हिन्दी and मराठी.

Is this the builder’s app?

No. It is not run by, or affiliated with, Runwal or the builder.

Can I use it in an emergency?

No. For a fire, a medical emergency or any danger to life, call the emergency services.
